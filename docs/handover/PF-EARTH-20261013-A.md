## Mission and user decisions

Create a credible, deterministic, extensible planet simulator as the foundation of a terraforming game. Start with a largely/molten early planet; evolve through energy and phase-driven solidification, crust, volcanic/geologic change, volatile outgassing, steam/clouds/rain, real basins/oceans/lakes/rivers, climate/snow/ice, prebiotic chemistry and possible earliest life. Never present a uniquely known Earth history or guaranteed abiogenesis. Let uncertainty and alternative geological hypotheses later become meaningful gameplay. Scientific integrity, conservative evolution, good architecture, incremental releases and reproducible visual inspection are more important than artificial demo deadlines.

Focus on the **actual main 3D globe and local LOD 0–3**, not additional tabs, shader-only mock timelines or disconnected Hero scenes. User allows major refactoring if needed for credible outcomes; preserve already delivered terrain/erosion/river studies. They expect delegated execution, CI/CD, versioning, merges and reliable test requests only when visuals change.

## The identified technical root cause

`PlanetRegionalCoolingReference.CreateInitial` funds a 1600 K global rock layer using an effective phase material (`cp = 850 J/kg/K`, latent `400000 J/kg`, solidus `1300 K`, liquidus `1900 K`) and intentionally reassigns 1.2% of its existing enthalpy into one Gaussian-like hotspot (without net heat creation). Many cells remain partially molten; hotspot stands out. There is no physical globally molten initial state here.

`planetRenderer.js` orbit and local shader use `barrenRockMaterial` then an RGB orange blend weighted by surface Kelvin and melt fraction; one-zone uniform reference uses a different tint. The atlas itself is spatially smooth and bounded (PR #195) with quantized RGBA8 display data, not simulation authority. Real region heat exchange was implemented in #197 but has **zero conductivity configured in the current visual reference**; users won't yet see physical lateral redistributive cooling.

## What exists and what does not

- Exists: `PlanetOriginEpochState` canonical coupled ledger; `PlanetOriginSurfaceRockPhaseField` active spherical cells; thermally invertible material phase/enthalpy; deterministic projection; dry radiation; optional conservative lateral conduction; immutable/revisioned publication; WebGL globe and LOD; separate hydrology/terrain research. CI and Pages live smoke.
- Does **not** exist as a released coupled Earth-history scene: physically calibrated global magma circulation and solid crust growth; a real Hadean tectonic history; fully coupled volatile/steam-pressure/radiative atmosphere and cloud/rain; oceans formed from conserved condensate; coupled origin-of-life chemistry. Never imply one-zone/contrast references provide those.
- Model distinction: hypothetical *starting conditions* may be a chosen funded ensemble; *subsequent planet changes* must emerge from energy/mass/rock physics.
