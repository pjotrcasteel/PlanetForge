## Remaining physical/visual limits

The current default remains a **dry, airless ~7 m conducting shell**, not calibrated whole-Earth formation or deep convective magma. The initial sphere is physically smooth; fine lava currents, differentiated/coherent crust, crust transport and mountains are absent. Optical material parameters are explicit hypotheses, not calibrated spectral data. Do not fake these processes to make the globe attractive. Real PC/Safari appearance is a human gate. The workspace-only cold-start, cooperative-yield, larger-step and scratch-workspace changes above are not part of the deployed release. Plain JavaScript module cache invalidation still needs an upgrade-path audit. The browser remains a single-threaded WASM simulation; timer yields keep the browser event loop available between physical steps, but a single 384-column solve remains indivisible and device FPS/acceptable iPhone latency still require browser testing. Neither this bounded timestep nor the native benchmark establishes million-year chronology. Next implementation must move beyond a thin dry cooling kernel into physically funded planetary-scale mantle/volatile processes and mechanically supported surface evolution; next performance work should profile actual PC/mobile browser latency and consider worker offload while retaining atomic publication, cancellation, deterministic enthalpies and one clock.

Depth-refinement/conservation evidence remains in [solidification snapshot](PF-EARTH-20261013-A-20261013-solidification.md). Earlier releases, failures and architecture survive in [archived checkpoint history](PF-EARTH-20261013-A-20261013-pre-vertical-history.md) and the earlier dated handovers. Shader/detail texture must never substitute for funded physical heat, material or topography.

## Planet evolution vertical slice (local, not released)

The default view is now a continuous GPU planet-evolution model (`wwwroot/js/evolution/`), driven by one clock that accelerates by one decade per 3.5 s up to the selected Myr/s cap:

- `thermalHistory.js` — 0-D mantle thermal history: magma-ocean crystallisation (radiative/convective flux, crust lifetime), rheological transition → stagnant lid with heat-pipe share → plate-tectonics onset (~1.4 Gyr) as the mantle cools.
- `crustRafts.js` — CPU quench-crust rafts on the convecting magma ocean; rafts founder, then weld as crystal fraction rises.
- `planetEvolution.js` — GPU ping-pong lithosphere (1024×512, ~39 km/texel): plate ids, crustal age, felsic thickness, edifice, mafic pile, strain. Processes: rigid Euler-pole plates (poles ~90° from tracked plate centres), rank-based subduction polarity, continental collision (thickening saturates ~70 km), gap filling with new oceanic crust, arc magmatism, plate-local crust relaxation, plumes/hotspot tracks, heat-pipe vents, Hadean impact flux (Marchi-style decay, N(>D)∝D⁻², Pike crater scaling), TTG felsic crust from thick mafic roots, Parsons–Sclater/Airy isostasy. Every few bakes a CPU pass keeps each plate's largest connected component and accretes detached fragments to the surrounding plate (prevents ribbon artefacts); rifting splits the largest plate and reuses consumed plate ids.
- Display liberties are listed in the HUD (`DEMO_LIBERTIES`): seam widening ×500 (real glowing cracks are metre-wide, an honest view is a dark ball), relief exaggeration, sub-grid procedural detail, auto-exposure.

Physics answer recorded for the user: an airless magma ocean skins over within about a minute; glow is visible only through cracks/foundering seams, so a uniform tint change was a missing model, not reality.

## Earth history regime (local, not released)

From 1.8 Ga the emergent planet hands over (40 Myr cross-fade) to `wwwroot/js/evolution/earthHistory.js`, driven by the offline pyGPlates bake of Cao et al. 2024 / Merdith et al. 2021 (`tools/earth-bake/bake.py` → `wwwroot/data/earth/`, ~9.5 MB, 5 Myr frames, 579 plates). GPU passes compute ocean formation age (semi-Lagrangian, ridges from plate splits), trenches/arcs, collision and rift uplift from Euler-pole closing rates, present-referenced continental relief, and derived elevation. From 540 Ma relief is nudged (τ 5 Myr) toward the PALEOMAP PaleoDEMs (Scotese & Wright 2018), present-referenced offline by `tools/earth-bake/paleodem.py` (`paleo.bin`, 4.3 MB); before 540 Ma the baseline is the compressed 540 Ma DEM. The final 30 Myr assimilate toward ETOPO 2022 so the run ends on today's map. Sea level in this regime is pinned to the DEM datum (0). HUD shows the era (Nuna, Rodinia, Gondwana & Laurussia, Pangaea, …) and `EARTH_LIBERTIES`. Verified locally: Phanerozoic maps follow the PALEOMAP atlas, land 14–24 % in the past, 29.1 % today; formation spec 4/4 (Edge). Fixed: PaleoDEM uploads used to rebind the active texture unit (tectonics sampler), producing a spurious 4 km plateau on Africa before 540 Ma. Before 540 Ma continental blocks are floored at +0.12 km and narrow inter-block polygon gaps are bridged (removes the deep 'channels' through Rodinia).
